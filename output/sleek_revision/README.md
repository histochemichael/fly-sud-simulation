# Impulse Neuro - polished silent video

fly_ethanol_reward_sleek.mp4 is the latest visual revision (3:20, 1080p,
25 fps, no audio). sleek_examples.zip contains all eight condition/phase
clips. Earlier video versions remain unchanged in their own folders.

Changes: a transparent blue waveform logo, aligned lightweight blue header,
subtle overall progress line, a navy frame around the publication-style
results panels, and six-frame (0.24 s) fade-ins on static chapter cards.
Recorded training/test movement is never blended or faded. Chapter timing,
data, circuit overlays, caveats, and reported statistics are unchanged.

The first 26 seconds introduce the experiment. The main film includes all
four conditions, paired-versus-DAN comparisons, complete post-choice paths,
individual choice dots at 2:20 and latency violins at 2:30. The final sections
explain findings, paper limitations and proposed next steps. The existing
16-page report retains the same scientific content and statistical table.

The logo extraction used the built-in image editing tool; see
logo_edit_prompt.md. The supplied black-background source remains intact.
The extracted PNG has genuine alpha, composited rather than flattened.

All observations derive from the existing original 400-fly batch and four
filmed representatives. No new experiment or statistical calculation was
performed for this styling revision. The original exact-prefix audits and
outcome statistics are unchanged. No upload was performed.

Circuit graphics remain illustrative, not anatomically registered axons or
measured imaging. JFRC2 anatomy: Virtual Fly Brain, Jenett et al. 2012 and
Ito et al. 2014, CC BY 4.0; see the report for attribution and limitations.
Paper: Kaun et al. 2011, Nature Neuroscience 14:612-619, doi:10.1038/nn.2805.
